UNO.NET ERP

An ERP With an AI Mind.

UNO.NET is our accounting and inventory ERP, built on Microsoft .NET and SQL Server for the Lebanese and Middle-East market. It works online, offline, or both at once with automatic synchronization. And now every number in it can be questioned, explained and forecast by AI.

📒

Financial Accounting

General ledger, journal wizards, receipt and payment vouchers, difference of exchange, posting, accounting periods and a flexible chart of accounts that fits any country in the region. Balance sheet, cash flow and income statement to international standards.

AIAsk for any balance, ratio or unusual voucher in plain language and get a grounded, explainable answer.
🤝

Receivables & Payables

Third-party management for clients, suppliers and salesmen, with dual-identity accounts for customers who also supply you. Commission rules and quota management included.

AIAged receivables, DSO and DPO, customers who went quiet, and a win-back list ready for your sales team.
📦

Inventory & Warehouses

Unlimited barcodes and units per item, sub-items, serial numbers, multiple warehouses, price lists, barcode labels and an inventory photo gallery. Sales, returns, purchases and transfers between warehouses.

AIDemand forecast, stockout risk, reorder suggestions grouped by supplier, slow-moving and dead stock.
🧾

Sales & Purchasing

Invoices, quotations, returns and purchase orders with barcode-reader entry, discounts, landed cost and price-change wizards. Entry fast enough for a counter, detail deep enough for an accountant.

AITop and worst sellers, margin per product and per customer, basket analysis, seasonality and supplier price increases.
💱

Multi-Currency & VAT

Every transaction carries three currencies: local, USD and foreign. Accounts can be dynamic or fixed currency. Lebanese VAT handled natively, including exempt and Article 59 treatment.

AIThe VAT return, filing schedule and a reconciliation of declared VAT against actual sales, in one question.
🏢

Cost Centers & Branches

Cost center classification with allocation at voucher level, location management across branches, and several companies in one database with centralized settings.

AIProfit per branch and per cost center, budget versus actual, and the assistant always asks which company you mean.
📊

Reports, Dashboards & BI

Interactive reports with on-screen filters, drill-down from a statement line to its voucher, a report designer, watermarks, eight export formats and a dashboard built for CEOs and top management.

AINarrated insights: ask the assistant to explain a chart, compare two periods or write the monthly summary.
🔐

Security, Audit & Recovery

User groups with permissions down to screens, reports and functions. Audit control tracks every change. Server-date enforcement, accounting period locks and auto-recovery of unsaved work.

AIThe assistant is read-only by design. It never writes to your books, and every question it answers is logged.
🔄

Online, Offline or Both

Run on your own dedicated server, in the cloud, or on a workstation with no permanent internet. Our synchronization option keeps both in step. Updates are free with a yearly maintenance plan.

AIA secure replica of your data feeds the AI assistant a few times a day, so advice stays fresh even for offline sites.
UNO AI ASSISTANT

Talk to your books.

An auditor and financial advisor that sits on top of your live ERP data. Ask in Arabic or English, get an answer with the numbers behind it. Ninety-eight purpose-built queries cover accounting, cash, receivables, inventory, VAT, cost centers, cheques and forecasting.

What is our cash position today? Which customers are 90 days overdue? Prepare the VAT return for Q3 Which items run out in 30 days? Compare this month to last year Who stopped buying from us? Is our balance sheet balanced? Forecast next quarter's sales

Leading Innovation

Ask the Right Questions Before You Buy

WebShortcut is always seeking new ways to present the latest innovations. Request a demo to see our advanced technology in action. And when investing in any new solution, ask the vendor these questions first.

When investing in a new solution, the buyer should ask:

  • Are you really using the latest technology?
  • Are you using a real database server? Is my data, my most valuable asset, really secure?
  • Can the software answer my future needs? What if I need more features in the coming years?
  • What if my data becomes huge over the years, or the number of users doubles?
  • What is the cost of future updates? Are there any hidden costs?
  • Am I getting a reasonable and fair pricing model?
  • Can AI answer questions about my data without that data leaving my premises?
  • Can the software explain its numbers, and does the AI ever write to my books without approval?
  • Will the AI features keep improving as models improve, at no extra cost?

UNO.NET answers yes to all of them.
